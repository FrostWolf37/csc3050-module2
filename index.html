Featured Video Games

A quick look at my favorite games.

Action / Greek Mythology

Hades II

Released: September 25, 2025

Battle through Olympus and the Underworld as Melinoë(Daughter of Hades) to defeat Chronos, Titan of Time and save her family.

Key Features

  • Wield dark magic and witching arts beyond the Underworld
  • Battle past the forces of Chronos, the Titan of Time
  • Use Mythical Boons to aid you from Olympic Gods, like Zeus and Poseidon
Adventure / Survival

Elden Ring

Released: February 24, 2022

Explore a haunting, broken world ruled by corrupted demigods and forge your own path to become the new ruler of the Lands Between.

Key Features

  • Vast open world of the Lands Between
  • Challenging boss encounters with intense combat mechanics
  • Deep build flexibility for melee, sorcery, and incantations
MMO-RPG

World of Warcraft

Released: November 23, 2004

Join millions of players across Azeroth, and take on legendary dungeons, raids, and world-shattering threats.

Key Features

  • Massive world spanning dozens of continents and expansions
  • Diverse characters customization across multiple races
  • Play any of the 13 unique playable classes from Warriors, Paladins, Mages, Druids, Hunters, etc!
